Abstract
Background:  Artificial  intelligence  (AI)  increasingly  shapes  university  studentsÆ  interpersonal
communication  and  emotional  engagement,  influencing  intimacy  development  in  digitally  mediated
relationships.  However,  gender-based  differences  and  contextual  moderators  remain  insufficiently
synthesized.
Objective: This study systematically reviewed and meta-analyzed empirical evidence on the association
between  AI-mediated  communication  and  intimacy  development  among  university  students,  with
particular attention to gender differences and moderating factors.
Methods: Thirty-two studies involving 18,457 participants were included. A comprehensive search of
major academic databases was conducted from January 2021 to December 2025. Study selection, data
extraction,  and  quality  assessment  were  independently  performed  by  two  reviewers.  Random-effects
meta-analyses,  subgroup  analyses,  meta-regression,  sensitivity  tests,  and publication bias  assessments
were conducted.
Results:  The  pooled  analysis  demonstrated  a  moderate  positive  association  between  AI-mediated
communication and intimacy development (r = .27, 95% CI [.25, .29]). Stronger effects were observed
among  female  students  (r  =  .33)  than  male  students  (r  =  .24).  Age,  cultural  context,  AI  system  type,
duration of use, and platform characteristics significantly moderated effect sizes. Findings were robust
across analytical models, with minimal evidence of publication bias.
Conclusions:  AI-mediated  communication  plays  a  meaningful  role  in  shaping  intimacy  among
university students, with notable gender and contextual variations. Balanced, emotionally responsive AI
use appears to support relational development, whereas excessive reliance may pose psychosocial risks.
These  findings  highlight  the  importance  of  gender-sensitive  digital  literacy,  ethical  AI  design,  and
targeted interventions in higher education.

Keywords: Artificial intelligence, intimacy development, gender differences, university students, digital
relationships, meta-analysis

4. Discussion
This

comprehensive evidence that artificial intelligence-mediated
communication  is  significantly  associated  with  intimacy
development  among  university
students.  Across  32
quantitative studies involving more than 18,000 participants,
a  moderate  positive  pooled  effect  was  observed,  indicating
that AI engagement is generally linked to greater emotional
closeness,  relational  satisfaction,  and  perceived  partner
responsiveness. These findings are consistent with previous
research suggesting that digitally mediated environments can
self-disclosure,  emotional  accessibility,  and
enhance
relational maintenance when used in balanced and supportive
ways (Brandtzaeg et al., 2022; Chaturvedi et al., 2024) [8, 10].
However,  several  studies  also  indicated  that  excessive
reliance on AI may contribute to emotional displacement and
reduced face-to-face interaction, highlighting the dual role of
technology in intimate relationships (Depounti  et al., 2023)
[13].
Clear  gender-related  patterns  emerged  across  the  included
studies. Subgroup analyses demonstrated that the association
between  AI-mediated  communication  and  intimacy  was
significantly  stronger  among  female  students  than  among
male  students.  This  trend  aligns  with  established  literature

emotionally  oriented

These findings support gender socialization and attachment-
