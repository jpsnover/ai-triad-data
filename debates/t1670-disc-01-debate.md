### Accelerationist (accelerationist)

Permissionless innovation accelerates capability and safety simultaneously by flooding the world with decentralized problem-solvers before monolithic bad actors can corner the market on harm. Open ecosystems remain the only empirical mechanism proven to distribute power, break monopolies, and outpace bad actors through rapid, transparent iteration.

Maintaining meaningful human agency over high-consequence technologies requires durable boundaries between public safety research and unconstrained capability spread. Societies that dismantle these institutional safeguards in the name of speed surrender their ultimate defense against irreversible technological breakdown.
